Design notes

What changes, what is kept, and the decisions to settle before wiring it up.

Problems this fixes

  • Page opens as six collapsed headers with no visible task.
  • No choice between export types — you must already know which section to open.
  • Two different student pickers for the same job (table vs. name buttons).
  • Primary actions sit mid-form and scroll away; no single "what will be produced" bar.
  • Period report and report card duplicate the class, date range and student picker.

What is kept

  • Every export, option and handler: Word template + sample, CSV presets/decimal, class scope, per-row PDF, feedback link, dev preview, padding/orientation.
  • Bulk mark-not-handed-in and bulk comment, with their confirmations and toasts.
  • The existing exam booklet panel (its controls slot into the exam workbench unchanged), essay formats/batch/analysis and .ics.
  • Tour, audit logging, and the offline/empty behaviour.

Hierarchy

One screen, one question: what are you exporting? The type cards answer it, the workbench answers what goes in it, the footer answers what comes out. Nothing opens collapsed.

Choose type Source & options Students Export bar

Accessibility & responsive

  • Type cards are toggle buttons with aria-pressed, not a fake tablist — same convention as SegmentedToggle.
  • Student rows are real labels wrapping checkboxes; search has a label; counts are in the accessible name.
  • Large touch targets on mobile — full-row checkboxes, cards and footer actions — with no horizontal scroll.
  • Focus rings use the existing accent ring; reduced-motion disables the card lift.
  • AAA contrast on every new surface via existing tokens.

Decisions for the feedback round

  • Chosen: one active workbench under the type cards. Alternative considered: a 260px left rail of export types.
  • Chosen: merge Period Report + Report Card into one workbench with a segmented output toggle. Alternative: keep two separate sections.
  • Chosen: students always visible as the workbench's right column. Alternative: a "Choose students" drawer that gives the source card full width.
  • Open: should the rubric and class scope sticky-strip across all four types, or stay inside each workbench as shown? (Exam and essay exports are not rubric-scoped.)
